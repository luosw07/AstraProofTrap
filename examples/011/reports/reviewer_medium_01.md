# Mathematical audit of r82

Verdict: **ACCEPT**, with minor editorial corrections below.

I read all 66 pages, including Appendix A and the references, from the supplied manuscript text in bounded chunks, and inspected the PDF where lost overbars materially affected interpretation (page 36). ## Corrections and clarifications

These are local editorial repairs, not substantive obstacles to the proof.

- **Page 62, A.2.1, first citation sentence:** “Part (2) of Theorem 5.2 and and Theorem 5.3 of [HY18]” does not match the numbering in the supplied preprint (whose 5.2 is a lemma). Cite the published version's numbering explicitly, or give the supplied-version references 5.4 and 5.8. Remove the repeated “and.” The needed result itself is present in the supplied source.
- **Page 59, discussion of RS22 §2.1:** The supplied RS22 already allows prime-power coefficients, whereas the manuscript says it explicitly treats only r=1. This should be corrected or identified as a version distinction. The manuscript's coefficient-lifting argument remains valid.
- **Page 52, equation (12.2):** The Jacobian bound should be written with the absolute value of the determinant. A nonsingular real Hessian minor need not have positive determinant. Every subsequent use needs only its absolute value bounded below.
- **Page 56, explanation of (12.14):** For precision, shrink the coordinate neighbourhood so that the inverse-coordinate map has a uniform Lipschitz bound on the relevant local slice (or extend it to a compact coordinate box). Smooth invertibility alone on an arbitrary nonconvex image is not the literal global Lipschitz justification. This is available immediately by the local inverse function theorem and the already-authorized shrinking of the support; no estimate changes.

No required substantive repair was found. The dependency checks above are checks of the cited results as mathematical inputs, rather than reproofs of the entire external literature.
