## Local corrections and a proof clarification

These are not substantive objections to the conclusions.

- **Corollary \`cor:timeindependentdevelopment\`, item (3), lines 2537–2573:** The proof passes from completeness of q = exp(2f)h to a discussion of maximality without explicitly proving that the full stationary product is globally hyperbolic. Completeness of the flow of X alone would not establish this. In this specific metric, however, the missing argument is short. Put b = exp(f)e_u, so |b|_q = 1 and
  exp(2f)g = q − dt⊙b.
  A causal tangent a∂_t + V satisfies |V|_q² ≤ 2a b(V), hence |V|_q ≤ 2|a|. Also dt is timelike. Parametrizing a consistently oriented causal curve by t gives spatial speed at most 2. If t had a finite endpoint, completeness of q would give a spatial limit, allowing causal continuation. Thus every inextendible causal curve has t-range all of R and meets every slice once. This proves the claimed Cauchy property. The author should insert this argument before the maximality discussion; it repairs the presentation without changing the theorem or requiring a stronger hypothesis.
